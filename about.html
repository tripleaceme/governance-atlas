About the project

The open reference for data protection law

Privacy law shouldn't require a law degree to look up. Governance Atlas turns every country's data protection regime into a page you can read in six minutes — and trust.

Why this exists

If you're expanding a product into Ghana tomorrow, you don't want to read a 120-page act. You want a trustworthy summary, the key obligations, links to the official law, and a way to compare it with the countries you already operate in. That resource didn't exist — especially for Africa, where a wave of new data protection laws has arrived in the last decade with far less coverage than the GDPR gets.

Governance Atlas exists for the startup founder scoping a new market, the analytics engineer asked "are we compliant?", the DPO managing five jurisdictions, and the student trying to understand how the world regulates data.

How the data is maintained

Every country is a structured data file in an open GitHub repository — the law, the regulator, the obligations, the sources, and a last-verified date. The website is generated from those files, so the country pages, comparison tables, and statistics can never drift apart.

When a law changes, anyone can open a pull request with the correction and a link to the official source. Every fact on this site should be traceable to a primary source — if you find one that isn't, that's a bug.

What this is not

Governance Atlas is not legal advice. Summaries are written for orientation, not for compliance decisions. Laws change, interpretations differ, and your situation is specific — always verify against the official sources linked on every page, and consult a qualified lawyer for decisions that matter.

Who builds it

Governance Atlas is built by Ayoade Adegbite, a data engineer working across analytics engineering and data governance. You can find them on LinkedIn.

Contributions are welcome — country verifications, corrections, translations, and new official sources are the most valuable. The project lives at github.com/tripleaceme/governance-atlas.
