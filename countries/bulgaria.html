Executive summary

Compiled from public sources · not legal advice

Bulgaria is an EU member state where the GDPR applies directly. The national Personal Data Protection Act (originally 2002) was substantially amended in 2019 to implement the GDPR's member-state options and transpose the Law Enforcement Directive. Bulgaria lowered the child consent age for information society services to 14 and added national rules on employment-context processing, such as limits on copying identity documents and retention of job applicant data (6 months by default).

The supervisory authority is the Commission for Personal Data Protection (CPDP), a five-member collegial body with its own budget and staff. The CPDP was an early mover on large GDPR fines: in 2019 it imposed roughly EUR 2.55 million on the National Revenue Agency for a massive breach caused by inadequate security measures, and it continues to issue significant penalties alongside a high volume of complaint decisions.

One distinctive episode: in late 2019 Bulgaria's Constitutional Court struck down the amendment's ten-criteria balancing test for journalistic processing as unconstitutional, so freedom-of-expression balancing is now assessed case by case. For businesses, compliance is standard GDPR compliance plus the Bulgarian employment-law specifics and the age-14 consent rule.

Key obligations

  • 01
    GDPR core compliance — Apply GDPR principles, lawful bases, data subject rights and accountability duties, which apply directly in Bulgaria.
  • 02
    Employment data rules — Observe national restrictions on copying ID documents and the default 6-month retention limit for unsuccessful job applicants' data.
  • 03
    Breach notification — Notify the CPDP within 72 hours of becoming aware of a reportable breach; notify individuals when risk is high.
  • 04
    Child consent verification — Obtain parental consent for information society services offered to children under 14.
  • 05
    DPO appointment — Appoint a DPO where GDPR Article 37 triggers apply and notify the CPDP of the designation.
