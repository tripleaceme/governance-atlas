Executive summary

Compiled from public sources · not legal advice

Bosnia and Herzegovina replaced its outdated 2006 Data Protection Law with a new, GDPR-aligned Law on the Protection of Personal Data, adopted by the Parliamentary Assembly on 30 January 2025, published in the Official Gazette of BiH No. 12/2025, in force from March 2025 and applicable from 4 October 2025. The reform transposes the concepts and structure of both the GDPR and the Law Enforcement Directive and is a key step in the country's EU accession alignment.

The new law introduces GDPR-style data subject rights, mandatory Data Protection Officer appointments, Data Protection Impact Assessments, uniform rules on international transfers, and 72-hour breach notification to the strengthened Personal Data Protection Agency (AZLP). Penalties jump dramatically from the old law's token fines to GDPR-level exposure — up to 4% of global annual turnover or BAM 40 million per authoritative summaries.

As of August 2026 the law is fully applicable, but enforcement practice under the new regime is still young: the AZLP is building capacity and issuing guidance, and organisations that were compliant only with the 2006 law face substantial uplift work. Bosnia and Herzegovina does not hold an EU adequacy decision.

Key obligations

  • 01
    GDPR-aligned lawful processing — Establish a lawful basis for each processing activity and honour the full set of GDPR-style data subject rights introduced by the 2025 law.
  • 02
    Breach notification — Report personal data breaches to the AZLP without undue delay and within 72 hours of awareness; notify affected individuals without undue delay where required.
  • 03
    DPO appointment — Appoint a Data Protection Officer where the law's mandatory triggers apply.
  • 04
    DPIA for high-risk processing — Conduct data protection impact assessments before high-risk processing operations.
  • 05
    Transfer compliance — Apply the new uniform international-transfer rules (adequacy or appropriate safeguards) for any cross-border data flows.
