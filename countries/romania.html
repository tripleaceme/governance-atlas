Executive summary

Compiled from public sources · not legal advice

Romania is an EU member state where the GDPR applies directly, supplemented by Law No. 190/2018 (in force 31 July 2018). The national law adds conditions for processing national identification numbers (CNP), genetic, biometric and health data, workplace monitoring rules, and a distinctive lighter-touch regime for public authorities: ANSPDCP first issues a warning and a remediation plan, and fines on public bodies are capped at RON 200,000.

The framework also includes Law No. 102/2005 establishing the supervisory authority, Law No. 363/2018 transposing the Law Enforcement Directive, and Law No. 506/2004 on ePrivacy in electronic communications. The authority, ANSPDCP, is an autonomous body based in Bucharest that issues secondary regulations on DPIAs, breach reporting and certification.

Workplace monitoring is a notable Romanian specificity: employee monitoring via electronic means or CCTV requires a legitimate-interest justification, prior consultation, and is only allowed if less intrusive methods have not proven effective. Enforcement is regular though fines are typically modest compared with Western European authorities.

Key obligations

  • 01
    GDPR core compliance — Apply GDPR principles, lawful bases, data subject rights and accountability duties, which apply directly in Romania.
  • 02
    National ID number safeguards — Processing the CNP (personal numeric code) based on legitimate interests requires specific safeguards such as a DPO, retention limits and staff training.
  • 03
    Workplace monitoring limits — Electronic monitoring of employees requires demonstrated legitimate interest, prior information/consultation and proof that less intrusive means were insufficient.
  • 04
    Breach notification — Notify ANSPDCP within 72 hours of becoming aware of a reportable breach.
  • 05
    DPO appointment — Appoint a DPO where GDPR Article 37 triggers apply; also expected when relying on legitimate interest to process national ID numbers.
