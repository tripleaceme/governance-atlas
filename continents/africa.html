Continental & regional frameworks

Laws and models that apply above the national level.

African Union · Binding treaty

Malabo Convention (2014)

The AU Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection — entered into force June 2023 after Mauritania's 15th ratification. The continental baseline for member states.

Read summary →
ECOWAS · Binding on 15 states

Supplementary Act on Personal Data Protection (2010)

West Africa's binding regional framework — the reason most ECOWAS states legislated early. Directly applicable within member-state legal orders.

Read summary →
SADC · Model law

SADC Model Law on Data Protection (2013)

A non-binding template Southern African states adapt into national legislation — visible in the laws of Zambia, Zimbabwe and Eswatini.

Read summary →
EAC · Framework

EAC Framework for Cyberlaws (2008)

Early guidance for East African Community members on privacy and e-transactions legislation.
