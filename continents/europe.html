Continental & regional frameworks

Laws and models that apply above the national level.

European Union · Binding regulation

General Data Protection Regulation (2016)

Directly applicable in all 27 EU member states and the EEA (Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein). Extraterritorial reach makes it the de-facto global benchmark.

Read summary →
Council of Europe · Binding treaty

Convention 108+ (2018)

The modernised data protection convention — the only binding international treaty in the field, open to accession by non-European states.

Read summary →
European Union · Directive

ePrivacy Directive (2002)

Governs cookies, electronic communications, and direct marketing across the EU — implemented through national laws; a replacement regulation has been under negotiation for years.

Read summary →
EDPB · Guidance

European Data Protection Board

Coordinates the EU's national supervisory authorities and issues guidelines that shape how the GDPR is interpreted and enforced in practice.
