Templates

The documents your regulator will ask for

Seven working documents covering the paperwork every data protection programme needs. Each comes as an editable Word file and a PDF, with guidance notes explaining what the document is for, when it is required, and where organisations usually get it wrong.

Used for your receipt and to reach your download page again if you lose the link. Nothing else is sent to it.

What you are buying. A template, not legal advice. These documents are a structured starting point drafted against the common shape of data protection regulation — they are not tailored to your country, your sector, or your organisation. Complete them for your own circumstances and have them reviewed by a qualified adviser before you rely on them or sign them. Licensed for use within your own organisation and with your clients; not for resale as a template. Because these are digital files delivered immediately, purchases are non-refundable once downloaded — but if a file will not open or is not what was described, tell us and we will put it right.
Governance Atlas is a reference tool, not legal advice. Laws change and the rules that apply depend on your organisation and its activities. Always check the official sources linked on each country page before relying on the information.
