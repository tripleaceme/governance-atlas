Executive summary

Compiled from public sources · not legal advice

Chad's Law No. 007/PR/2015 of 10 February 2015 establishes a comprehensive framework for personal data protection, part of a 2015 package of digital laws that also created the supervisory body. Unusually, supervision is not vested in a dedicated privacy authority but in the cybersecurity regulator: the Agence Nationale de Sécurité Informatique et de Certification Électronique (ANSICE), established by Law No. 006/PR/2015. After years of latency, ANSICE became fully operational in late 2020 and has since begun data controller registration and enforcement activity.

The law applies to businesses of all sizes processing data of people in Chad. There is no country-wide registration system as such, but any processing must be declared in writing to ANSICE, and prior authorization is required for genetic and biometric data and health-sector research. Cross-border transfers require informing ANSICE beforehand; transfers to countries outside the CEMAC/CEEAC zones are allowed only where the controller ensures sufficient protection of the data subject's rights. The Act contains no DPO requirement.

Sanctions combine fines of XAF 1 million to 10 million with imprisonment of three months to one year for offenders. Enforcement is real but modest — ANSICE's dual cybersecurity/privacy mandate and limited resources mean compliance pressure in Chad is lower than in Africa's larger markets, though registration and transfer formalities are actively administered.

Key obligations

  • 01
    Declare processing to ANSICE — Address a written declaration to ANSICE before processing personal data; obtain prior authorization for genetic, biometric and health-research data.
  • 02
    Inform ANSICE before transfers abroad — Notify ANSICE before any transfer of personal data outside Chad; transfers beyond CEMAC/CEEAC require sufficient protection of data subjects' rights and freedoms.
  • 03
    Process data lawfully and proportionately — Observe the Act's principles of lawful, purpose-bound and proportionate processing for all personal data of individuals in Chad.
  • 04
    Respect data subject rights and security — Honor rights of access, rectification and objection and secure data against misuse — violations expose the organization to fines up to XAF 10 million and imprisonment.
