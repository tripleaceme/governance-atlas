Executive summary

Compiled from public sources · not legal advice

Uzbekistan's Law No. ZRU-547 'On Personal Data', adopted 2 July 2019 and in force since 1 October 2019, is the country's first comprehensive data protection statute. It applies to state bodies and private operators alike, requires consent or another legal basis for processing, and obliges most operators to register their personal data databases with the Personalization Agency under the Ministry of Justice — a notification-style filing decided within 15 days, with exemptions for employment data, publicly available data and several other categories. Operators must also designate a responsible person or unit for data protection.

The law became internationally notorious for its 2021 data localization amendment, which required personal data of Uzbek citizens processed via information technologies to be stored on servers physically located in Uzbekistan, enforced through a special register and website blocking (applied at times to major foreign platforms). That regime was substantially dismantled by amendments in force from 27 March 2026: storage and processing abroad is now permitted provided information security requirements and international data protection standards are met, clearing the path for cross-border digital services.

Enforcement is administered by the Personalization Agency alongside telecom inspection bodies, with penalties that are modest in monetary terms (base-calculation-value fines and possible criminal liability) but operationally significant through blocking powers. For foreign businesses the practical agenda is database registration, appointing a responsible person, and tracking the new 2026 cross-border conditions.

Key obligations

  • 01
    Register databases — Notify and register personal data databases with the Personalization Agency before processing, unless an exemption (e.g., employment data, publicly available data) applies.
  • 02
    Designate a responsible person — Appoint a structural unit or responsible person to organize personal data protection, per the 2023 Model Rules on Processing of Personal Data.
  • 03
    Cross-border and storage conditions — Since March 2026, data may be stored/processed abroad only if information security requirements and international data protection standards are met; transfers otherwise rely on adequacy, consent or treaty.
  • 04
    Lawful basis and purpose limitation — Process personal data with the data subject's consent or another legal basis, for defined purposes, and destroy or suspend processing when violations occur.
  • 05
    Security measures — Implement technical and organizational measures to protect data; non-compliance can lead to inclusion in the violators' register and website blocking.
