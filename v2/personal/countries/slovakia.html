Executive summary

Compiled from public sources · not legal advice

Slovakia applies the EU GDPR directly, supplemented by Act No. 18/2018 Coll. on the Protection of Personal Data, adopted 29 November 2017 and effective 25 May 2018. Unusually, the Act restates much of the GDPR's text verbatim in Slovak law (to cover processing outside the GDPR's scope and transpose the Law Enforcement Directive), while adding national derogations: it keeps the children's online consent age at 16, regulates processing of birth numbers and employee data disclosure, and sets procedural rules for the supervisory authority.

The Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR) in Bratislava supervises compliance, conducts inspections under an annual plan, handles complaints, and issues decisions and methodological guidance. Enforcement is steady but fines are typically modest — usually in the thousands to tens of thousands of euros — with an emphasis on corrective measures.

Compliance is standard GDPR practice with Slovak specifics: transparency in Slovak, the 16-year consent age, restrictions on using the national birth number as an identifier, and a statutory rule allowing employers to disclose limited employee contact data. Because Act 18/2018 duplicates GDPR text, practitioners must track which provisions apply directly under the Regulation versus the national Act.

Key obligations

  • 01
    GDPR compliance baseline — Apply the GDPR in full: lawful basis, transparency, data subject rights, and accountability documentation.
  • 02
    Breach notification — Notify the Office within 72 hours of a personal data breach and affected individuals where risk is high.
  • 03
    Children's consent at 16 — Obtain parental consent for information society services offered to children under 16.
  • 04
    Restrict birth-number processing — Process the Slovak birth number (rodné číslo) only where law permits and never publish it — a national derogation.
  • 05
    Notify DPO appointments — Where a DPO is required, notify the appointment and contact details to the Office.
