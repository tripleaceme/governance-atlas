Executive summary

Compiled from public sources · not legal advice

As an EU member state, Greece is governed directly by the GDPR, supplemented since August 2019 by Law 4624/2019, which implements the member-state derogations and transposes the Law Enforcement Directive. The combined framework applies to any organisation established in Greece and to foreign organisations offering goods or services to, or monitoring, people in Greece. Law 4624/2019 also carried over rules in areas like employee data processing, which is a well-litigated topic before the Greek regulator.

Greece's notable national choices include setting the digital age of consent at 15 (parental consent required below that for online services), specific conditions for processing employee data, and a EUR 10 million cap on administrative fines against public bodies. Otherwise the standard GDPR machinery applies: 72-hour breach notification, DPOs where Article 37 triggers are met, DPIAs for high-risk processing, and the Chapter V transfer regime.

The Hellenic Data Protection Authority (HDPA), a constitutionally established independent authority based in Athens, is an active enforcer — its fines have ranged from a few thousand euros up to EUR 20 million, targeting telecoms, banks and employers for unlawful employee monitoring, transparency failures and security lapses. Companies operating in Greece should treat HDPA enforcement risk as comparable to other mature EU regulators.

Key obligations

  • 01
    GDPR compliance baseline — Maintain lawful bases, transparency notices, records of processing, DPIAs for high-risk activities, and honour data subject rights within one month.
  • 02
    Breach notification — Report personal data breaches to the HDPA within 72 hours and to affected individuals where the risk to them is high.
  • 03
    DPO appointment — Appoint a DPO if you are a public authority or your core activities involve large-scale monitoring or large-scale special-category processing.
  • 04
    Employee data safeguards — Follow Law 4624/2019's specific conditions for processing employee data, an area of frequent HDPA enforcement.
  • 05
    Children's consent — Obtain verified parental consent before relying on consent from children under 15 for information-society services.
