Executive summary

Compiled from public sources · not legal advice

Liechtenstein applies the GDPR through its EEA membership, supplemented by the national Data Protection Act (Datenschutzgesetz, DSG) enacted on 4 October 2018. The DSG implements the member-state opening clauses: it retains the GDPR's default age of 16 for children's consent to information-society services, regulates processing by public authorities, and sets national criminal and procedural provisions. Being a microstate deeply integrated with both the EEA and Switzerland, Liechtenstein is a common jurisdiction for financial-sector data flows.

Supervision belongs to the Datenschutzstelle, a small but functional independent authority that participates in the European Data Protection Board alongside EU regulators. Enforcement volume is naturally low given the country's size — guidance and complaint-handling dominate over headline fines — but the full GDPR sanction regime of up to EUR 20 million or 4% of worldwide turnover applies to private entities.

Compliance obligations mirror the GDPR everywhere in the EEA: lawful basis, transparency, data subject rights, 72-hour breach notification, DPIAs, DPO designation where triggered, and Chapter V transfer tools for exports outside the EEA. Its banking and fiduciary industries add sectoral secrecy duties on top.

Key obligations

  • 01
    Lawful basis and transparency — All processing needs a GDPR Art. 6 basis with clear privacy information to data subjects.
  • 02
    Notify breaches within 72 hours — Report personal data breaches to the Datenschutzstelle within 72 hours; inform individuals when risk is high.
  • 03
    Appoint a DPO where required — Public bodies and organisations with large-scale monitoring or sensitive-data processing must designate a DPO.
  • 04
    Keep records and assess risk — Maintain Art. 30 records of processing and run DPIAs for high-risk activities.
  • 05
    Frame international transfers — Transfers outside the EEA require adequacy, SCCs or another Chapter V safeguard.
