Executive summary

Compiled from public sources · not legal advice

Serbia's Law on Personal Data Protection (ZZPL), published in Official Gazette No. 87/2018 and applicable since August 2019, is closely modelled on the GDPR and unusually also incorporates the EU Law Enforcement Directive in the same statute. It brings GDPR-style principles, data subject rights, accountability tools (records, DPIAs, DPOs), 72-hour breach notification and extraterritorial reach over foreign controllers targeting people in Serbia. Serbia set the child consent age for information society services at 15.

The supervisory authority is the Poverenik — the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection — a well-established independent body that also handles freedom of information. The law's biggest weakness is its misdemeanour-based sanctions: fines are capped at RSD 2 million (roughly €17,000), far below GDPR levels, which limits deterrence even as inspection activity increases.

Reform is underway: the Government adopted a Personal Data Protection Strategy 2023–2030, and in January 2025 a working group began drafting ZZPL amendments to cover gaps such as cookies, video surveillance, biometrics and AI. As of August 2026 the 2018 law remains in force and the amendment timeline is uncertain. Serbia does not hold an EU adequacy decision; cross-border transfers are free to Convention 108 parties and listed adequate countries, with safeguards or Commissioner approval otherwise.

Key obligations

  • 01
    GDPR-mirrored compliance — Apply ZZPL principles and lawful bases and honour GDPR-style data subject rights, including for foreign controllers targeting Serbian residents.
  • 02
    Records and local representative — Maintain records of processing; foreign controllers within scope must designate a representative in Serbia.
  • 03
    Breach notification — Notify the Poverenik within 72 hours of becoming aware of a reportable breach; notify individuals when risk is high.
  • 04
    Transfer rules — Verify the destination is a Convention 108 party or on the adequacy list; otherwise implement the Serbian SCCs or obtain Commissioner approval.
  • 05
    DPO appointment — Appoint a Data Protection Officer where the ZZPL's mandatory GDPR-style triggers apply.
