Essential for records of processing and for answering deletion requests honestly.

Keep it accurate

Data quality & contracts

Accuracy is a legal principle in most privacy laws — these tools enforce it in your pipelines.

Quality · Open sourceGreat ExpectationsA framework for defining, running, and documenting tests that validate your data. Quality · Open coreSodaData quality testing driven by checks written in a simple, readable language. Standard · Linux FoundationOpen Data Contract StandardA vendor-neutral YAML specification for describing a dataset and the guarantees it offers. Guide + toolingdatacontract.comGuidance and command-line tooling for authoring, testing, and enforcing data contracts.
