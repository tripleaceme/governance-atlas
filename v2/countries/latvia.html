The short version

Compiled from public sources · not legal advice

Latvia applies the EU GDPR directly, supplemented by the Personal Data Processing Law, which entered into force on 5 July 2018 — making Latvia the first Baltic state to adopt GDPR-aligned national legislation, replacing its 2000 Personal Data Protection Law. The national law sets out the powers and procedures of the supervisory authority, lowers the age of children's consent for information society services to 13, and regulates specific contexts such as processing for journalistic, academic and official-purposes.

The supervisory authority is the Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija, DVI), operating since 2001 and now functioning as Latvia's GDPR authority under Cabinet of Ministers supervision. The DVI handles complaints, conducts inspections, and issues fines; enforcement activity and fine levels have historically been modest compared with larger EU authorities, but the DVI is active in guidance, DPO certification exams, and cross-border cooperation through the EDPB.

Compliance in Latvia is standard GDPR practice — lawful basis, Latvian-language transparency notices, 72-hour breach notification, DPIAs for high-risk processing. The main local particularities are the 13-year consent age for online services and national rules on personal identity codes and journalistic processing.

What you actually have to do

  • 01
    GDPR compliance baseline — Apply the GDPR in full: lawful basis, transparency, data subject rights, and accountability documentation.
  • 02
    Breach notification — Notify the Data State Inspectorate within 72 hours of a personal data breach; inform individuals where risk is high.
  • 03
    Children's consent at 13 — Obtain parental consent for information society services offered to children under 13 — Latvia adopted the lowest age permitted by the GDPR.
  • 04
    DPO where required — Appoint a data protection officer if Art. 37 GDPR thresholds are met; the DVI maintains a voluntary DPO examination and list.
  • 05
    DPIA for high-risk processing — Carry out impact assessments for high-risk processing and consult the DVI where residual risk remains.
