Peru

Primary law: Law No. 29733 — Personal Data Protection Law (2011), as implemented by Supreme Decree 016-2024-JUS — supervised by Autoridad Nacional de Protección de Datos Personales (National Authority for Personal Data Protection), under the Ministry of Justice and Human Rights (ANPD).

The short version

Compiled from public sources · not legal advice

Peru has had a comprehensive, GDPR-influenced data protection law since 2011 — Law No. 29733 — but the regime was substantially modernized by a new implementing Regulation (Supreme Decree 016-2024-JUS), published 30 November 2024 and in force since 30 March 2025. The law applies to public and private entities processing personal data in Peru and, under the new Regulation, extends extraterritorially to foreign companies that offer goods or services to people in Peru or monitor their behavior; such companies must appoint a local representative.

The framework is distinctive in the region for its mandatory registration of 'data banks' with the RNPDP and its consent-centric model: consent must be free, prior, express and informed. The 2024 Regulation added GDPR-style machinery — a 48-hour breach notification duty, revenue-threshold-based data protection officer requirements, and security measures aligned with ISO/IEC 27001.

Enforcement is handled by the ANPD, a directorate within the Ministry of Justice and Human Rights rather than a fully independent agency. It is an active regulator by regional standards, regularly fining companies for unregistered data banks, unlawful marketing and security failures, with penalties reaching 100 UIT for the most serious infringements. Peru does not hold an EU adequacy decision.

What you actually have to do

  • 01
    Register data banks — Controllers must register their personal data banks in the National Register (RNPDP) before processing; unregistered databases are a common basis for fines.
  • 02
    Obtain valid consent — Consent is the default legal basis and must be free, prior, express, unequivocal and informed; sensitive data requires written/express consent.
  • 03
    Notify breaches within 48 hours — Security incidents exposing significant volumes of data or causing serious harm must be reported to the ANPD and affected individuals within 48 hours under the 2024 Regulation.
  • 04
    Implement ISO-aligned security — Technical and organizational measures aligned with ISO/IEC 27001 standards are required to protect personal data.
  • 05
    Appoint DPO / local representative — Organizations above revenue thresholds must appoint a data protection officer, and foreign companies targeting Peru must designate a local representative.
