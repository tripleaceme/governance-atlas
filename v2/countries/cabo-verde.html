The short version

Compiled from public sources · not legal advice

Cabo Verde was the first African country to enact comprehensive data protection legislation with Law No. 133/V/2001 of 22 January 2001. The framework was reformed in 2013 (Law 41/VIII/2013, which restructured the supervisory regime) and substantially modernized by Law No. 121/IX/2021 of 17 March 2021, which pulled the regime toward GDPR standards: unambiguous affirmative consent, extraterritorial application to foreign controllers processing data of people in Cabo Verde, a 72-hour breach notification duty, and conditional DPO appointment requirements.

The supervisory authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados Pessoais (CNPD), operational since 2015, receives processing notifications, authorizes special-category processing, handles complaints and imposes sanctions. Cross-border transfers to countries without adequate protection are permitted only with CNPD permission, data subject consent, or specified necessity grounds.

The 2021 amendment sharply raised the stakes on enforcement, with first-offence fines ranging from CVE 1 million up to CVE 100 million. For a small island economy with growing outsourcing and tourism-related data flows, Cabo Verde now has one of the more modern regimes in West Africa on paper, though public enforcement actions remain relatively rare.

What you actually have to do

  • 01
    Notify the CNPD before processing — Notify processing operations to the CNPD and obtain authorization for special categories of personal data.
  • 02
    Report breaches within 72 hours — Notify the CNPD of personal data breaches within 72 hours of becoming aware of them (introduced by the 2021 amendment).
  • 03
    Obtain valid affirmative consent — Rely on consent expressed through a declaration or unequivocal positive act, in line with the 2021 GDPR-style definition.
  • 04
    Appoint a DPO where required — Designate a Data Protection Officer where the statutory conditions are met and communicate their contact details to the CNPD.
  • 05
    Safeguard international transfers — Transfer data to non-adequate countries only with CNPD permission, the data subject's consent, or another specified ground.
