The short version

Compiled from public sources · not legal advice

The recorded entry for Niger is out of date: Law No. 2017-28 of 3 May 2017 (as amended in 2019) was repealed and replaced by Law No. 2022-59 of 16 December 2022 on the protection of personal data, itself modified by Law No. 2023-31 of 4 July 2023. Law 2022-59 is now Niger's principal, comprehensive data protection statute, and the country therefore remains in the 'law in force' category — but citations should point to the 2022 Act.

The supervisory authority is the Haute Autorité de Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel (HAPDP). The 2022 Act introduced modern elements including a mandatory breach-notification duty: under Article 83, controllers must inform the HAPDP without delay upon becoming aware of a breach, and must inform data subjects as soon as possible where there is high risk to their rights and freedoms. Notably, the law contains no DPO appointment requirement. Non-compliance with obligations, including breach notification, is backed by criminal penalties (imprisonment and fines) under Article 98.

Enforcement reality is shaped by Niger's post-July 2023 political situation: the HAPDP has continued operating, with commentary through 2024 describing an authority balancing awareness-raising against control activity. Practical enforcement remains light, and detailed transfer and penalty specifics under the 2022-59/2023-31 framework are not well documented in accessible public sources.

What you actually have to do

  • 01
    Complete prior formalities with the HAPDP — Register/declare processing operations with the Haute Autorité before processing personal data in Niger.
  • 02
    Notify data breaches without delay — Inform the HAPDP as soon as a personal data breach is discovered, and notify affected data subjects promptly where the breach poses a high risk (Art. 83).
  • 03
    Process data lawfully — Observe the lawfulness, purpose and proportionality principles of Law 2022-59 for all processing of personal data.
  • 04
    Respect data subject rights — Honor individuals' rights of access, rectification and objection under the 2022 Act.
