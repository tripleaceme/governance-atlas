The short version

Compiled from public sources · not legal advice

Luxembourg applies the EU GDPR directly, supplemented by the Act of 1 August 2018 on the organisation of the National Data Protection Commission (CNPD) and the general data protection framework, effective 20 August 2018, which repealed the 2002 data protection law. A companion Act of the same date transposes the Law Enforcement Directive for criminal-justice processing. The national act keeps derogations light: it retains the GDPR default consent age of 16, adds conditions for employee monitoring and processing for scientific research, and exempts the State and municipalities from administrative fines.

The CNPD, based in Belvaux, supervises a market dense with banks, funds, and EU-headquartered tech companies — Amazon's EU headquarters made the CNPD its lead authority, producing the landmark €746 million fine against Amazon in 2021, one of the largest GDPR penalties ever issued (contested in the Luxembourg courts). Day-to-day, the CNPD favours audits, thematic investigations (CCTV, geolocation, DPO function) and published guidance.

Compliance is standard GDPR practice with local nuances around bank secrecy, employee monitoring rules under the Labour Code (works-council involvement and CNPD-relevant notice duties), and the practical significance of Luxembourg as lead supervisory authority for multinationals established there.

What you actually have to do

  • 01
    GDPR compliance baseline — Apply the GDPR in full: lawful basis, transparency, data subject rights, and accountability documentation.
  • 02
    Breach notification — Notify the CNPD within 72 hours of a personal data breach and affected individuals where risk is high.
  • 03
    Employee monitoring safeguards — Workplace surveillance must comply with Labour Code Art. L.261-1 conditions, including prior information of staff and employee representatives.
  • 04
    DPIA for high-risk processing — Conduct data protection impact assessments for high-risk processing against the CNPD's published list.
  • 05
    DPO where required — Appoint a data protection officer where Art. 37 GDPR thresholds are met and notify the CNPD.
