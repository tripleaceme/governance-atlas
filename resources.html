The toolkit

Put privacy into practice

Knowing what the law requires is only the beginning. You also need the right processes, tools, and documentation to put those requirements into practice.

The resources in this section can help you understand what data you have, how it moves through your organisation, and how to document your practices. These are general data management tools rather than privacy products, but each supports work that personal data protection requires.

A tool can support good governance, but no tool makes an organisation compliant by itself. Compliance depends on how your organisation uses these tools, the processes around them, and the requirements that apply to you.

Know what data you have

You can't govern data you can't see. A data catalog helps you understand what data your organisation holds, where it comes from, who is responsible for it, and how it is used.

Catalog · Open sourceDataHub ↗An open-source platform for discovering, documenting, and managing metadata about data across an organisation. Catalog · Open sourceOpenMetadata ↗An open-source platform for data discovery, metadata management, and governance. Discovery · Open sourceAmundsen ↗An open-source data discovery and metadata platform.

Understand where your data goes

Data lineage shows where data comes from, how it changes, and where it goes as it moves through your organisation. This can help you understand how personal data flows between systems, identify who or what uses it, investigate data quality issues, and support privacy activities such as records of processing and deletion requests.

Standard · Open sourceOpenLineage ↗An open standard for collecting and sharing metadata about data pipelines and their lineage. Lineage · Open sourceMarquez ↗An open-source metadata service that collects and visualises lineage information using the OpenLineage standard. Catalog · Open sourceDataHub ↗A data catalog that can also capture and display lineage between datasets and systems.

Keep your data accurate

Privacy laws often require organisations to take reasonable steps to keep personal data accurate and up to date. Data quality tools can help you find problems such as missing, invalid, inconsistent, or outdated data. They can also help you test data automatically and catch problems before they spread to other systems.

Quality · Open sourceGreat Expectations ↗An open-source framework for defining and testing expectations about your data. Quality · Open coreSoda ↗A data quality platform for checking data, monitoring quality, and detecting issues. Quality · Open sourcedbt tests ↗Built-in tests for checking common data quality conditions such as uniqueness, non-null values, and relationships between data.

Make data expectations explicit

A data contract defines what a dataset should contain and how it should behave. It can specify things such as the expected fields, data types, allowed values, ownership, and quality requirements. Data contracts help teams agree on what data producers should provide and what data consumers can rely on. They can also make it easier to detect changes that could affect downstream systems.

Standard · Bitol / Linux FoundationOpen Data Contract Standard ↗A vendor-neutral YAML specification for describing a dataset and the guarantees it offers. Guide + toolingdatacontract.com & Data Contract CLI ↗Guidance and command-line tooling for authoring, testing, and enforcing data contracts. EditorData Contract Editor ↗A browser-based editor for writing and validating data contracts.

Keep your documentation and policies up to date

Documentation is part of good governance. It helps people understand what data you have, how it is used, who is responsible for it, and what rules apply. Treating documentation and policies as code means keeping them in version-controlled files and updating them through the same workflows used for software. This makes changes easier to review, track, and audit.

Docs · Open sourcedbt Docs ↗Generates browsable documentation and lineage directly from your dbt project. Portal · Open sourceBackstage ↗An open-source developer portal for cataloguing services, ownership, and documentation. Docs · Open sourceMkDocs ↗A static site generator that turns Markdown files into searchable documentation. Policy as code · Open sourceOpen Policy Agent ↗A general-purpose engine for expressing and enforcing policy as code.
